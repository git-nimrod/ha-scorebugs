# Scorebugs 1.0.0

The first public release. TV-style scorebugs for Home Assistant: your teams' games drawn the way they look on a broadcast, from the
first always-on score boxes of the 1990s to this season's.

## What's in it

- **289 looks** across 12 sports: American football (NFL, college, CFL, UFL), baseball, hockey, basketball, soccer, golf, motor
  racing, tennis, MMA, Australian football, cricket and volleyball. See [Every look](../bugs.md).
- **Follow your teams** in **Configure > Add a team**: 198 leagues in 17 sports, each team with a sensor holding its current or next
  game (score, clock, period, down and distance, count and bases, power play, bonus, records, the network and more). See
  [Leagues you can follow](../leagues.md).
- **A Scorebug card** that adds itself to your dashboards. Its editor asks for your team, then shows every look as a live preview to
  pick from.
- **One look everywhere or one per team** (**Configure > Scorebug looks**), plus a **Look entity** per team for automations. "The
  network showing the game" draws each game in its broadcaster's look, where Scorebugs has one.
- **Looks that fit more than one sport.** Football, basketball and hockey looks draw each other's games; baseball looks draw football,
  basketball, hockey and soccer games too, with the period, the clock and one detail in place of the count, outs and bases. Looks made
  for one sport's layout (golf, tennis, racing, MMA, cricket, volleyball, Australian football) stay with their sport.
- **How bugs look, set once** (**Configure > How bugs look on cards**): logo versions, what goes in the network's spot (the Home
  Assistant logo, the league's logo, your own picture or nothing), an old-TV or VHS picture, playoff looks, panel textures and more.
- **Your own team logos** (**Configure > Team logo**): any logo ESPN has for the team, or a picture of your own.
- **Demo games** to try looks out with no game on.

## Good to know

- No network logo, wordmark or channel bug is drawn: the Home Assistant logo sits in the network's spot. See [NOTICE](../../NOTICE.md).
- Scores come from free public feeds (ESPN's, and the CFL's, the EuroLeague's and MLB's own for leagues ESPN lacks). They are unofficial
  and run a little behind the broadcast.
- Only the leagues of the teams you follow are ever asked for scores.
