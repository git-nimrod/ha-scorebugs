# Leagues you can follow

**Configure > Add a team** offers **198 leagues** in 17 sports. Pick the sport, then the league, then your
team (for golf, racing, tennis and MMA: the whole event, or one player from the current field). Each team gets a sensor with its
current game, and only the leagues of the teams you follow are ever asked for scores.

Leagues without a look of their own yet draw with their sport's looks; new sports (rugby, lacrosse, field hockey, water polo) draw
with a plain bar until their own looks are built.
